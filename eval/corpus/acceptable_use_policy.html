POL-0101: Acceptable Use of Company Devices

Personal Use

Limited personal use of company laptops and phones is allowed if it does not interfere with work, use excessive bandwidth, or break the law.

Prohibited Activities

Do not disable security software, share your sign-in details, connect unknown storage devices, or store company data in personal cloud accounts. Do not use peer-to-peer file sharing programs on company devices.

Monitoring

Company devices and networks are monitored for security purposes. Violations are reported to your manager and may lead to disciplinary action.
