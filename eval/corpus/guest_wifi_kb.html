KB0077: Office Wi-Fi Networks

Applies to: all company offices.

Corporate Network

Company-managed laptops join the CORP network automatically using a device certificate. No password is needed or shown. If a managed laptop does not join, restart it while in the office so it can renew its certificate.

Guest Network

Visitors and personal phones use the GUEST network. Guest access codes are issued per visitor by the host employee through the visitor registration system and expire at midnight. Codes are never posted on walls or sent in bulk email.

Slow or Dropping Wi-Fi

Move closer to an access point, turn Wi-Fi off and on, and forget and rejoin the network. If a whole floor is affected, report it to the Service Desk with the floor and room number.
