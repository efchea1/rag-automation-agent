POL-0112: Multi-Factor Authentication Enrollment

Applies to: every corporate identity account, including contractors.

Enrollment

New accounts must register an authenticator app within 7 days of first sign-in. SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary fallback for 14 days. Hardware security keys are required for administrators and finance staff.

Lost or Replaced Phone

If you lose your phone or get a new one, contact the Service Desk to clear your registered methods. The agent confirms your identity through your manager before issuing a one-time bypass code valid for 8 hours, during which you re-register.

Sign-In Prompts You Did Not Expect

Never approve a sign-in prompt you did not start. Repeated unexpected prompts are reported to Security as possible MFA fatigue attacks, and the account is temporarily blocked until Security reviews it.
