POL-0131: Change Management

Applies to: changes to production systems, network devices, and shared infrastructure.

Standard and Normal Changes

Pre-approved standard changes can proceed without review. Normal changes are submitted at least three business days ahead and approved by the Change Advisory Board, which meets every Tuesday.

Emergency Changes

An emergency change restores a production service during an active incident. It may be approved by the on-duty change manager by phone and documented within one business day afterwards. Emergency changes still need a rollback plan.

Freeze Periods

No normal changes are made during the last week of each financial quarter.
