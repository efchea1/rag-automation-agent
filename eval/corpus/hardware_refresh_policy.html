POL-0120: Laptop Refresh and Replacement

Refresh Cycle

Standard laptops are replaced every four years. Engineering and design roles are replaced every three years. IT contacts you one month before your refresh date.

Damaged or Broken Laptops

Report damage to the Service Desk the same day. A loaner laptop is provided while yours is repaired. Accidental damage is covered once per refresh cycle; repeated damage requires manager approval for a replacement.

Data on Old Laptops

Old laptops are wiped by IT before disposal. Save your files to the company cloud drive before handing a laptop back, because local files are not recovered.
