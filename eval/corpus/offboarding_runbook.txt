Employee IT Offboarding Runbook

Purpose
This runbook covers removing access when an employee leaves the company.

Steps
1. HR records the leaving date, which creates an offboarding task for IT.
2. On the last working day at 5pm local time, the identity account is disabled and all active sessions are revoked.
3. Mailbox access transfers to the manager for 30 days, then the mailbox is archived.
4. The employee returns the laptop and badge; remote employees receive a prepaid return box.
5. Licenses are reclaimed and reassigned within 7 days.

Immediate Terminations
For involuntary terminations, HR notifies IT directly and access is disabled at the time HR specifies, not at end of day.
