POL-0107: Incident Severity and Escalation Policy

Applies to: all incidents logged with the IT Service Desk, including incidents raised automatically from monitoring alerts.

Severity Levels

  • Sev 1 (Critical): a core service is down for most or all users, or there is an active security incident. Examples: company-wide email outage, VPN gateway unavailable for all remote staff, suspected ransomware.
  • Sev 2 (High): a core service is degraded, or a single site or department cannot work. Example: one office cannot reach the file server.
  • Sev 3 (Medium): one user or a small group is affected and a workaround exists. Example: a single laptop cannot print.
  • Sev 4 (Low): questions, how-to requests, and cosmetic issues.

Response Targets

  • Sev 1: acknowledge within 15 minutes, update stakeholders every 30 minutes.
  • Sev 2: acknowledge within 1 hour, update every 2 hours.
  • Sev 3: acknowledge within 4 business hours.
  • Sev 4: acknowledge within 2 business days.

Escalation Path

  1. Tier 1 Service Desk owns every incident at intake and assigns a severity.
  2. Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents page the on-call engineer for the affected service immediately; do not wait for Tier 1 troubleshooting to finish.
  3. If a Sev 1 incident is not mitigated within 60 minutes, the on-call engineer escalates to the IT Operations Manager, who opens a bridge call.
  4. Any incident involving a suspected security breach is escalated to the Security team at once, regardless of severity.

After the Incident

Every Sev 1 incident requires a written post-incident review within five business days, covering timeline, root cause, customer impact, and follow-up actions with named owners. Sev 2 reviews are at the manager's discretion.
